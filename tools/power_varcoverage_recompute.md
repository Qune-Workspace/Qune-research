**Reviewer's reading (summarised).** A 60-month forward Kupiec record (1,137 scored days) detects 99% under-coverage at 80% power only if the true exceedance rate is about 1.98% or higher. That is roughly the 2019–2026 rate; the in-sample rates of 1.6–1.9% fall short. At 95% the test has essentially no power against any seen rate (0.06–0.40). A forward test would therefore be worth pre-registering only for 99% coverage, ideally over 120 months (threshold 1.63%), and with a clustering-robust test.
