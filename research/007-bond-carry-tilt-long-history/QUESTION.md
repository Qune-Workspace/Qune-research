## 1. The question, and why this form

The agenda's question 2 was "bond carry, and carry plus trend, in Treasuries". On the ETF era it cannot be a test: the C2-bar MDE at w = 0.5 over 185 months is 2.20% a year for IEF against bills and 4.55% for TLT (`power_next.txt`), above the 2% bar. The only form inside the bar is the long constant-maturity history on Study 006's frozen synthetic 7–10-year leg (MDE 1.52% a year at w = 0.5 over 593 months), and on that leg the binary carry-sign switch is already published as Study 006's trial T3 (Study 001's "carry including roll-down" rule; Sharpe 0.468, Newey–West t 1.91 against B3, C2 fail). So the rule here must use what the sign does not: the **level** of carry.

**Decision: a carry tilt by level, plus its combination with the 12-month trend rule.** The question:

> **Does scaling a long-only intermediate-duration position by the level of its carry (yield over bills plus roll-down, as defined by Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen and Vrugt [KMPV18]) improve risk-adjusted return over the same average exposure held throughout, after realistic costs, on 1974–2018; and does adding the 12-month trend rule to it add anything the carry level does not already give?**

Primary rule **T1, carry tilt by rank.** At each month end the carry of the 7–10-year leg is c_t = (DGS10 − bond-equivalent DTB3) + D_mod(DGS10) × (DGS10 − DGS7)/3, exactly Study 001 §7's T3 signal, read from the FRED files Study 006 already holds. The weight in the duration leg over the next holding period is the percentile rank of c_t among all month-end carries observed so far (expanding window, at least 60 observations), w_t ∈ [0, 1]; the rest is in Study 001's cash leg. No threshold, no scale, no smoothing, no volatility scaling: the only choice is the 60-month minimum history, fixed here before any carry is computed.

Why the rank and not a fixed scale: a linear map w = clip(c/κ, 0, 1) needs a κ, and any κ chosen now would be chosen with the author's general knowledge that carry was larger in absolute terms before 1990 than after; the rank has no κ and adapts to the era without a look-ahead. Why expanding and not trailing: a trailing 120-month rank would make the first usable signal 1979 and discard the decade in which Study 006 found its edge (§2.3 says why that matters); an expanding rank starts after 60 months (first signal month-end 1974-06, first holding period July 1974) and has no second window length to choose. Its cost is stated now: in 1974–1979 the rank rests on five to ten years of history and is noisy, as it would have been for an investor at the time.

Why this one, and what it would teach either way: Study 006 tested whether the *sign* of the spread (and of carry) times duration; the result was a near miss carried by 1969–1981. A level tilt asks a different question, whether a steeper curve means more and a flatter curve less, which is the predictive-regression reading of [CS91], [CP05] and [KMPV18] rather than a switch. If T1 passes where 006's T3 did not, the level carries information beyond the sign; if it fails while 006's T1 passed, the inversions of the 1970s were the whole story and the level adds nothing, which is [TV12]'s reading. Both are results.

## 2. Trials, family count and the fragility rule

### 2.1 Registered trials (N = 5)

| ID | Rule | Why registered |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | carry tilt by expanding rank, minimum 60 months (primary) | the question |
| T1a | the same with a minimum of 36 months | fragility companion: the one choice in T1, moved down |
| T1b | the same with a minimum of 120 months (its window starts five years later; reported on T1's window from its own first signal and on the common sub-window) | fragility companion: the one choice in T1, moved up |
| T2 | 0.5 × T1's weight + 0.5 × 1[12-month trend of the leg over cash > 0] | the agenda's "carry plus trend", the standard rates book [BGHLS15] |
| T3 | 1[12-month trend of the leg over cash > 0], Study 003's rule unchanged | the attribution T2 needs, and the agenda's separate long-history trend candidate, merged here |

Study 006's fragility rule is kept **for T1 only**: "supported in history" for T1 needs T1a and T1b not "not supported" on the same criteria, because a tilt whose verdict depends on how many months of history the rank uses is the case the rule was written for. T2 and T3 are separate hypotheses, not readings of T1's signal; each gets its own verdict row, labelled secondary, and no cross-trial rule is applied between them and T1. The headline of the note is T1's verdict; T2's and T3's are reported under it, never promoted to the headline whatever they show.

The 12-month trend signal in T2 and T3 is Study 003's rule word for word (hold the leg when its trailing 12-month total return exceeds that of cash), computed on the synthetic leg's and cash's own monthly returns, which are instrument returns, not rule returns. Both are computed by the same code path as Study 006's benchmarks B0 and B1 (construction frozen at `a2b713b`, as 006 §0).

### 2.2 Family count, and the deflated Sharpe ratio decides at it

This is the second study on the same leg and the same window after the first's results were seen, and the trend trials overlap with published trend results on IEF. A reader is right to ask the second pass to pay for the first. **Decision:** criterion C3 (deflated Sharpe ratio ≥ 0.95) is decided at the **programme-level family count**, not at this study's N; the N = 5 and N = 10 figures are reported beside it. The family is every trial of a duration-timing rule on the 7–10-year Treasury leg over a window overlapping 1974–2018 whose return had been seen when this study runs: Study 006's three (spread sign, 21-day mean, carry sign; 1969–2018), Study 003's three (12-month trend and its two registered variants on IEF, 2003–2018) and this study's five, **11 at registration**, re-counted from the desk's registry and the public trial logs immediately before the history run, as 006 did (any addition raises it). Study 004's portfolio trials, which hold IEF as one of ten assets, are not counted; the note says so. At 534 months the DSR is barely sensitive to N (006's at N = 10 was 0.997), so this costs little and is stated for the principle, not as a safeguard that would bite.

### 2.3 Power, restated in both units (the agenda's rule, step 2 as amended on 2026-10-25)

Committed inputs: 006's leg volatility σ = 7.75% a year over 593 periods (`results/volcheck/volcheck.json`), the Newey–West to i.i.d. factor 0.972 (006 observed 0.963), K = 2.0 + 0.842, and the product form sd(d) = sd(w − w̄) × σ. Nothing new is read.

- **Window.** The first holding period starts in July 1974 (sixty month-end carries from 1969-07) and the last ends at the first execution date of January 2019: about **534 periods**, the exact count fixed by the data step. The 1969-08 to 1974-06 periods are outside the test for every trial, so all five share one window.
- **Sharpe units** (leg-independent): the C2 test can see a mean differential of **0.207** Sharpe at sd(w − w̄) = 0.5 over 534 months (0.196 at 593); at the sd of a uniform rank weight, 0.289, the dispersion of d is smaller and the figure is **0.120**.
- **Return units**, at σ = 7.75%: **1.6% a year** at sd(w − w̄) = 0.5 (the conservative figure that binds; the leg's volatility over 534 months is assumed equal to its 593-month figure until the data step measures it, and T2 and T3 can reach the 0.5 bound) and **0.93% a year** at the uniform-rank dispersion. The smaller figure is not better power: a weight that stays near 0.5 can also carry only a smaller edge, which is why the Sharpe-unit statement is the one to read.
- **What the test cannot see:** an edge of 1% a year or less at the conservative dispersion; the study says so in its first paragraph. **Forward record:** 60 months, same forms, about 4.8% a year or 0.62 Sharpe at sd 0.5 (scaling by √(534/60)); the pre-registration gives the exact figures.

The volatility check of 006 §2.3 is repeated once on the 534 periods before any rule return (bar 10.18% a year, unchanged, from `tools/power_candidate6.txt`); above it the study is labelled a low-power check and loses its "supported" row, as 006 would have been.

## 3. Design problems, and the decisions that resolve them

### 3.1 The window has been seen, and the form was chosen after seeing it

Study 006 published T1–T3, the decade differentials, the sub-periods and the mechanism check on this leg over 1969–2018 (its `README.md`, charts and `results/history/`), and the author read them. The long-history form of this question was chosen at 09:22Z on 2026-10-25, six hours after 006's history run, because power forced it, not because of what 006 showed; but the author cannot prove that from the record, and a reader should treat the test window as **in-sample for the family**, which is what §2.2's family count and the standing rule of 2026-10-24 (DSR at the programme count; only forward records are strictly out of sample, [METHODS §4](../../METHODS.md)) say. Mitigations, all checkable: no parameter of T1 is a scale or threshold that could have been set from 006's results (the rank has none; the 60-month minimum is the smallest round figure that covers one rate cycle and is moved both ways by T1a and T1b); T3 is 003's rule unchanged; T2's weights are fixed at one half each; the exposure-matched benchmark removes what 006 already showed about the leg's premium by era; the episode and forward windows are handled as in 006.

What the author has seen that bears on each trial: for **T1**, the sign of carry on this leg (006's T3: Sharpe 0.468, t 1.91) and the months the curve was inverted (006's spread chart, 77 of 593 month ends), so the sign component of the tilt is known and only the level component is open; for **T3**, the 12-month trend rule on IEF over 2003–2018 (Study 003 in-sample: Sharpe 0.45 against 0.55, t 0.37), on 2019–2026 (003 out of sample, t 1.31, one exit in 2021–22) and inside Study 004's portfolio (per-asset attribution: the signal hurt IEF in 2004–2018 and helped in 2019–2026), and 006's gate shows the synthetic leg tracks IEF closely over 2003–2018 (G1 0.008% a year, G2 0.131), so **the 2003–2018 part of T3's path is effectively known and only 1974–2002 (about 340 months) is open**; for **T2**, both of the above. The note's first paragraph carries this disclosure.

### 3.2 The gate, extended to a fractional weight

006's gate statistic G1 = |mean((h − w)·g)| × 12 is the bias the synthetic series puts into the decision quantity. For a fractional weight it is the same expression with w_t in place of h_t and w̄_o the overlap mean; G2 = sd(g)/sd(x_ETF) does not depend on the rule and was 0.131 on the same 192 overlap periods, so it is **inherited as observed**, not re-run as if new. **Decision:** G1 is computed once for each of the five weight paths on 2003–2018 from signal paths and instrument returns only (no rule return formed), with 006's bounds unchanged (G1 ≤ 0.18% a year); the trial whose G1 fails is dropped before any return, logged as "inconclusive (gate)", and the others proceed; if T1's fails, the study halts as inconclusive. The import-graph test of 006 (the gate module imports nothing from the backtest module) is kept. What the pass cannot show is unchanged: fidelity before 2003 is an assumption, and over 2008–2018 a rank weight is not constant, so G1 for T1 will rest on more than 006's twelve cash months but on the same single ETF.

### 3.3 Costs, turnover and the "low-powered" label for a fractional rule

Costs follow Study 001 §9.2 (base 2 bp a side, stress 4 bp) charged on the traded fraction |w_t − w_{t−1}| of equity each month; a full switch costs what it cost in 006. Study 003's switch-count rule becomes a turnover rule: if a trial's total one-way turnover over the window is below 8 units (eight full switches), its row says "low-powered". The exposure-matched benchmark B3 for each trial holds that trial's mean weight w̄ throughout, computed from signals only and frozen (006 §8.1), so each trial has its own B3; B1 (hold the leg) and B2 (50/50) are common.

### 3.4 Which windows test the rule

- **1974-07 to 2018-12, synthetic throughout (about 534 periods): the test.** Criteria C1–C4 of Study 001 §9.5 at base costs, C3 at the family count (§2.2). Sub-windows, descriptive, reported not selected from: 1974-07 to 2002-12 and 2003-01 to 2018-12; 006's three rate eras (1974-07 to 1981-12, 1982-01 to 2008-12, 2009-01 to 2018-12).
- **2019-01 to 2026-09: an episode, not a test**, run once after the note, no decision weight, actual IEF and BIL with the synthetic series alongside, as 006 §8.2. Disclosed now: the trend part of it is already published (003's out-of-sample run and 004's attribution), so for T2 and T3 the episode is a recomputation on a known window and is labelled as such.
- **The forward record: the only clean test.** Shadow only, no paper order; it starts at the first month end after publication (2026-10-30 if the study is published before 21:15 UTC that day, otherwise 2026-11-28), is scored once after 60 periods on the sign of mean(T1 − B3) and its Newey–West t, and publishes interim figures as "not yet scored", exactly 006 §11. The fractional weight is written to the record to four decimals with the carry, its rank and the number of months behind the rank.

**Verdict table (for the pre-registration's §9.5).** For T1: "supported in history; forward record pending" needs C1–C4 at base costs, C3 at the family count, the volatility bar met, and T1a and T1b not "not supported"; "not supported" if any of C1–C4 fails; "inconclusive (fragile)" if T1 passes and a companion fails; "inconclusive (gate)" if the gate fails. For T2 and T3 the same rows without the companion clause, labelled secondary. Precedence as 006 (not supported > inconclusive > supported). Nothing is called supported without "in history; forward record pending" until the forward record is scored.

## 4. Why not the alternatives

- **Carry across maturities (a 2-year or 20-year leg against the 7–10-year).** It needs constructions that do not exist, with the fidelity problem that halted Studies 001 and 002, and the maturity-switch MDE on the ETF era is 4.1–5.0% a year; it stays off the list until a leg other than 7–10 years has passed a gate.
- **Carry on the ETF era (IEF, TLT, SHY against BIL).** Above the bar (§1); and the 2019–2026 window is spent for every duration-timing rule on IEF ([METHODS §2.5](../../METHODS.md#25-out-of-sample-once-on-one-shared-window)).
- **A carry-sign switch, or carry with a dead band or a z-score.** The sign is 006's T3; a dead band or a z-score adds a scale that would be chosen after seeing 006's results.
- **Trend alone as its own study.** It would be a third study on the same leg with a third of its window already known; here it is one registered trial that serves the attribution, which is the honest size of that question.
- **The term-premium control from an affine model [ACM13].** Reported, not traded, in the agenda's wording; it needs a fitted model and its own data, which would be a second construction with its own gate. Deferred, named in the note's "what this study does not do".
