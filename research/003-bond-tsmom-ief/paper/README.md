## Rules that do not bend

- **Never backdated.** Every record carries `run_at_utc` and is committed on the day it is written; the commit timestamp is the second clock. A record for a signal date is written after that date's close, never before.
- **Never rewritten.** `write_record` refuses to overwrite. A correction is a new file with a note pointing at the old one.
- **Same code as the backtest.** The decision uses `src/signal.py`, `src/calendar.py` and `src/alpaca_bars.py` unchanged. A change to `src/` after publication is a dated amendment (§11.4) and the record before and after it is kept apart.
- **Desk limits win where tighter.** The sleeve's §12 limits sit under the desk's `RISK_FRAMEWORK.md`; where the two differ, the tighter one applies and the clearance note says which.
- **The engine stop is an overlay.** If the desk's required stop is triggered, the trigger is logged and the no-stop rule's record is computed alongside (§11.4).
- **A switch is one rebalance** (decided 2026-10-22 after the Tester's finding S3). The 0.5%-from-mid review on the sell leg holds the *next month's* order, not the buy of the same session: holding the buy would leave the sleeve in dollars on a fill that has already happened, which is a position the pre-registration does not have.
- **No other strategy holds IEF or BIL in this paper account while the Study 003 sleeve runs** (decided 2026-10-22 after the Tester's finding S7). The runner refuses with an alert when the account holds either symbol and the sleeve state holds nothing: that is the lost-state protection working, and it cannot tell the sleeve's shares from another strategy's. Study 001, the only other approval for these symbols, is halted and holds nothing.
- **The state is written before every order is sent** (pending order, then the fill). A pending order that was never confirmed refuses every later run until the owner has reconciled `paper-log/state.json` with the account by hand; the runner also refuses when the account's IEF and BIL positions differ from the state. A repeat order is impossible by construction; a stuck sleeve is possible and is alerted.
- **Open contract (Tester S4):** a day-one "signal unavailable" record cannot yet be superseded by a re-check record the runner would read; until the re-check schedule is built, such a month has no order and the record says so.

## Running the decision (from the study folder)

```
python -m paper.decide --signal-date 2026-10-30 --holding BIL --fetch
```

Credentials come only from the environment variables `src/alpaca_bars.py` reads; nothing is read from a file in this repository. `--dry-run` prints the record without writing it.
