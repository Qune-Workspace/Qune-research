**The gate [O].** For the first time the gate is open on the hike leg: the market's 0.885 lies outside [1.000, 1.000], the method's reading departs from the mid by +0.115, and R2 is true, so the printer's candidate line reads `method=fomc_bills method_p=1.000 range_lo=1.000 range_hi=1.000`, and the reading's row on [`readings.csv`](readings.csv) carries `gate=open`. The no-change leg is not a call: the reading 0.000 departs from 0.019 by less than 0.10. The calibration beside both: 74 scheduled meetings 2017-07..2026-10, Brier net 0.298 against climatology 0.529 and persistence 0.622, R2 true. Reported, not a rule: in the reading's own bin (0.9–1.0) a hike followed on 0.789 of 19 meetings; in [0.4, 0.8) the net reading over-read by 0.319 on 4 meetings (R3).

**Why there is no call [I].** An open gate is the condition under which the desk *may* log a call; the call itself is the desk's own two-decimal probability, and it must depart from the market by 0.10 with a basis sentence that says what the market is missing. The desk's probability of a 25 bp hike is **0.85**, a departure of 0.035, so no call. The reasons, in order: (1) the method's 1.000 is a ceiling, not a confidence: with 1.4 steps priced the reading says the bills price at least one hike, and it would say the same at 2 steps; it carries no information about how surely. (2) The method's own history in that bin is 15 hikes in 19 meetings, 0.789, with a one-standard-error width of about 0.09 on 19 cases; the market's 0.885 is one standard error above it, which is not a stated, testable reason that the market is wrong, in either direction. (3) The desk knows nothing about this meeting beyond the bills and the market's price: the 28 October statement's "accumulation of more information" and the two hawkish dissents read as they did last week, and the week's prints (the 2-year +15 bp, the bill +9 bp, [`../desk-view/DESK_VIEW.md`](../desk-view/DESK_VIEW.md)) moved with the market, not against it. The desk's 0.85 sits between the bin frequency and the market for the same reason as last week's 0.70 sat between 0.789 and 0.665; the market has crossed to the other side of the bin frequency and the desk's reading has followed it. For the record: hike of 50 bp or more **0.09** (the bills' 1.4 steps are where that leg's price comes from, and the desk has no reason to differ from 0.0915), no change **0.05** (against 0.019; the four meetings in a decade on which a fully priced hike did not come are why the desk's figure is above the market's, and 0.031 is not a call), cuts 0.01 together. `ledger.csv` stays empty.

**What moved this week [O].** The market's price of a hike rose from 0.665 (6 November) to 0.885 while the bill reading stayed at its ceiling and the spread behind it rose from 15 to 24 bp over funds; the 50 bp leg rose from 0.0195 (3 November) to 0.0915 and the no-change leg fell from 0.325 to 0.019. **What would make a call on this event next Friday [I].** A market below 0.785 with the bills still pricing a full step, where the stated reason is the bin frequency of 0.789 and the departure from the desk's 0.85 would be 0.10 or more; or a market above 0.985, where the stated reason is the four meetings in nineteen on which a fully priced hike did not come; or a stated reason from the November data releases themselves, written before the market moves on them.
